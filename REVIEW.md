# Adversarial review of the queen domination manuscript

## Corrections and evidence

| Finding | Resolution and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| The reported 79×, 165×, and 92× speedups divided Davies’s published CPU times by worker wall times. Other ratios mixed machines, solver configurations, proof-checking costs, or differently defined cases. | Removed the comparison table, the prototype timing and ablation claims, and projected runtimes for larger boards. Section 7.2 distinguishes the timing definitions and gives a paired certification-overhead experiment on a single machine. Davies’s Table 2 and the public $n=46$ records agree on 40,728 instances and the rounded published solve and checker totals. The archive fields `solve_cpu_s` and `check_cpu_s` in this project are elapsed sums, as established from the worker timers in `src/queen_prover.cpp`. |
| The draft overstated the disjointness of the split and left several thresholds in the encoder unproved. | Section 6 gives a covering theorem, the even-sided parity-counter bounds, and extensions to the auxiliary variables. In the maximal even zero-deficit frame, the missing cap needs a separate argument, including $k=2,3$. The specialized zero-deficit monochromatic formula can overlap a general branch. The proof includes the branch representatives and the branch-preserving lex constraints. |
| The draft used an informal argument for the border-waste identity in an odd-sided square with even $k$. The one-diagonal-deficit presentation left the signed offset implicit under interchange of the two diagonal families. | Sections 5.4–5.5 derive the conditions from the color identities and distinguish which family has one fewer occupied line. The identity used to omit the redundant bound holds when the box diagonal of the given color is occupied. |
| Two current DIMACS hashes at $n=50$ differed from the original audit logs. | The changed leaves are the two branch-8 formulas in `results/replay_n50/tags.txt`. Omitting only the one-deficit monochromatic waste-bound block from the encoder reproduces both archival hashes. The revised encoder adds constraints justified by the frame identities. Both current formulas were independently checked with the retained pairs, and the original logs remain intact. |
| The draft referred to the JSONL evidence as proof certificates and suggested broader proof coverage than the files provide. | Section 7.1 distinguishes the records from the DRAT files. The verifier reconstructs case and branch tags, validates record structure and hashes, and directly checks the two retained pairs when asked. The remaining original DRAT files were deleted after checking and are regenerable. |
| The draft did not credit an earlier computation of the independent domination values. | Neuhaus’s dissertation, §2.4, pp. 17–18, reports all five exact values. Östergård and Weakley already give independent upper bounds at 34, 38, 40, and 44 in their 2001 paper. Theorem 1.2 credits Neuhaus and uses explicit checked placements plus the ordinary lower bounds in *Hint and Conquer* and Davies as an alternative verification. Priority for the placements themselves was not established. |
| The original bibliography named T. Rostami as the author of *Hint and Conquer*. | The manuscript on the named GitHub branch has no byline, and Davies’s bibliography records it as anonymous. The citation now credits the manuscript on that basis. |
| The initial figure and evidence presentation were absent or difficult to read. | Figure 1 is a vector illustration connecting the empty-line frame with the two parity classes. The abstract, archival-results table, notation, reproduction links, and author disclosure were revised. Gemini 4 Neon through Google Antigravity is credited with the original research, calculations, implementation, and draft. The contribution statement distinguishes coordination and revision. |

## Mathematical checks

- **Sections 2–3.** Checked nonempty frames, margin capacities, the eight actions on lines and colors, geometry of side intersections, all corner multiplicities, and the dependence of the side formulas on the relation between $w$ and $h$.
- **Section 4.** Re-derived the intersecting intervals in the margin argument, the injection into occupied interior rows, the Hall capacity inequality, both nonsquare parity exclusions, and the odd square row/column-deficit exclusion.
- **Section 5.** Checked the five deficit categories and all parity subcases against the branch generator. Re-derived the fixed color counts, side offsets and zero-waste cases, the exceptional monochromatic frame, and the shifted linear and quadratic moments.
- **Section 6.** Checked line occupancy equivalences, dominance clauses, zero-deficit cardinalities, non-needy and double-coverage terms, unary counter overflow, offset count equality, modular residues and lexicographic comparisons. In the reduced grid, checked which shifted diagonals have fewer than two border hits. The coverage theorem explicitly extends a placement to a Boolean assignment.
- **Small boards.** Exhaustive bit-mask enumeration found 12, 4, and 0 dominating $n/2$-sets for $n=4,6,8$. The prover agreed with and without within-case lex constraints. On $n=8$ both runs checked every refutation.
- **Upper bounds.** All 12 stored witnesses passed direct checks, including the five independent witnesses. The eight coordinate lists printed in the manuscript agree with the original lists and JSON data.

The independent Raghavan–Venkatesan article is *Information Processing Letters* 25(5), 281–284. Its publisher record was accessible, and its lower-bound attribution was checked against Cockayne, Östergård–Weakley and the frame papers. ## Remaining limits

The complete fresh DRAT replay has not been run during this revision. For leaves outside the fresh checks, the evidence is the original recorded checker result and its match to the regenerated CNF. The historic logs lack dependency hashes, solver configuration and worker count fields. The build pins and environment documentation apply to the checked reproduction. No paired benchmark against Davies’s encoder is reported. The mathematical coverage proof has been audited against the source and checked on small boards, and remains a written argument.
